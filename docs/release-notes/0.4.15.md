# Omarchy News Radar 0.4.15

## Highlights

- YouTube stays quieter: descriptions lose sponsor, course, affiliate, and URL sludge; only substantive Omarchy activity enters the lane; one channel can hold two slots.
- Front Page still admits at most three Omarchy News items, but spends that quota on distinct topics so a Foundation/patronage cycle cannot consume the edition. Core keeps every official news item.
- Tune can hide Core, Plugins, or YouTube on this machine. Front Page, For You, and Saved stay reachable. YouTube Settings reuses the existing time window as TIME RANGE.
- Core, and Front Page Omarchy News / Core stories, read as a two-pane newspaper: a quiet headline list (title + date) beside a dominant article pane (large title, `date · source`, divider, full 0.4.14 body, then compact actions). Validated HTTPS article links are accent-colored and clickable. TYPE/TRUST/AUDIT live in collapsed Details. Plugin and YouTube cards stay denser where that density helps.
- The Keys legend is `Keys · ?` until opened and no longer shows a collapsed shortcut dump. The newspaper refresh timer no longer goes silent after a failed or empty helper run.

## Notes

- Local state moves from v10 to v11 to store `preferences.sectionVisibility`. Valid v1–v10 states migrate atomically and default every hideable rail to visible.
- Feed schema remains v2. YouTube ranking, views/likes/recent interleave, and the Front Page exclusion are unchanged.
- Language is never a drop reason. Critical reviews remain eligible.
