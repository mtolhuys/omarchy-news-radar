## Root cause and repair

The bar previously counted every unread event in the validated feed, while the newspaper projected stories through persistent per-section filters. With **With images** enabled for Plugins, unread image-less plugin stories could remain in the raw bar count even when no section exposed them. The badge therefore advertised unread work that the current newspaper could not reveal.

The badge now counts the deduplicated union of unread event IDs surviving the persistent filters for Front Page, For You, Core, Plugins, and Saved. The bar reloads the same exact enabled-plugin IDs used by the panel before every coalesced indicator request, so Front Page and For You do not retain a lifetime-stale enablement snapshot. Search text and pagination stay transient and do not alter the badge.
