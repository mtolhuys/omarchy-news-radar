## Archive policy and deliberate boundaries

Version 0.1 publishes the bounded live edition as `events.json` and as a same-byte monthly archive projection named from `generatedAt`. Each static-tree build is transactional; version 0.1 does not claim cross-deployment retention of earlier monthly files.

No public repository, remote, Pages site, tag, release, marketplace submission, external announcement, or host desktop activation was created. Quickshell still exposes its shared `org.quickshell` Wayland app ID, so Radar does not globally shadow or replace the shared desktop entry. Instead, the exact local AltTab and Omadock patches consume Radar's narrowly declared identity and deliberately refuse missing, disabled, malformed, or ambiguous matches. The Community channel remains intentionally editorial: production contains no reviewed records yet, and no unreviewed remote stream was substituted for it. The remaining distribution gates are owner authorization for the public-clone scenario and publication or upstream acceptance of the two companion patches before ordinary plugin updates can deliver the system-icon fix.
