## Root causes and product decisions

- GitHub's single hourly schedule was a best-effort trigger and stopped being delivered even though manual collection and Pages deployment remained healthy. Version 0.1.4 registers four explicit off-peak schedule opportunities, never cancels an active publication, preserves manual recovery, and separately records source check, collection, artifact publication, Pages propagation, and client cache time. Publication becomes visibly stale only after 90 minutes.
- The background-window glitch was not duplicate input. `toggle` interpreted an already-hosted obscured window as a close request, and invalid direct activation calls could leave hosted/compositor state half-open. Bar, shortcut, and Apps entry now use one summon-to-focus contract and one validated compositor helper; deliberate close routes remain explicit.
- **Refresh** implied upstream collection that the client cannot perform. It is now **Check for updates**: `R` and middle click fetch only the bounded published edition and report exact adopted-story count, no newer edition with publication age, stale publication, invalid data, or offline last-known-good state.
- `ListView.Contain` parked a variable-height keyboard selection at the lower clip boundary, and the asynchronous mark-read projection restored that clipped position. Crossing the bottom now records an explicit viewport anchor and eases the complete selected row to the top; subsequent Down movement is ordinary and does not move the viewport until the next crossing.
- The newspaper based refresh due-ness on edition collection time and used a fixed 30-minute timer. Starting near the age boundary could therefore defer discovery toward an hour, while watching only reading state left the badge stale after feed replacement. The visible newspaper now records the last actual check, schedules 15 minutes after success or five minutes after failure, and watches the atomic feed cache for immediate canonical unread/health propagation. It remains a passive badge: no desktop pop-up, sound, notification-center entry, daemon, or hidden-widget polling was added.

## Deliberate boundaries

- GitHub schedules remain best effort and may be delayed or dropped. Multiple opportunities and visible stale state reduce impact; they do not create a delivery guarantee.
- Marketplace submission starts review; only marketplace maintainers can approve and promote the exact commit, and marketplace verification is not a security audit.
- The optional visible newspaper is the passive unread/health surface. Hiding it stops network checks; version 1 does not interrupt the user with desktop notifications.
- Normal removal preserves cache, reading state, and saves. Explicit purge and the documented shortcut/launcher removal order remain intentional owner actions.
