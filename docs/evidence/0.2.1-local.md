## Root cause and repair

The normal reader exposed internal edition, source, cache, publication, and version diagnostics even when valid news was available. That was operational telemetry presented as product content. Successful reading now shows news and actionable state only; a concise recovery message remains visible when no validated news is available.

The remaining pagination glitch had two concrete causes. Replacing the JavaScript-array ListView model recycled every visible delegate, causing a transient displacement during **Load more**. The following per-story read projection then reassigned every payload and could relayout unchanged rows above the selection. Radar now appends to a stable rendered `ListModel`, retains unchanged payloads and delegates, keeps the adjacent viewport instantiated for keyboard geometry, and preserves explicit anchors across bounded rendered frames.
